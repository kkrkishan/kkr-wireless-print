Why Print Ofo?

No more emailing documents to yourself or waiting for WhatsApp Web to load.

Features

Built for Speed, Reliability & Privacy

Everything you need for seamless wireless printing with zero maintenance.

⚡

Zero-Config UDP Auto Pairing

Automatic discovery over UDP port 8089. Even if your router assigns a new local IP to your PC, the mobile app automatically finds and reconnects without re-pairing.

🖨️

Native Windows Spooler

Taps directly into the Windows Native Spooler API (winspool.drv). If your computer can print to the printer, Print Ofo can print to it with full hardware acceleration.

🔒

100% Offline & Private

Zero cloud servers, zero account sign-ups, and zero tracking. Your personal photos and private documents remain strictly inside your local Wi-Fi network.

🔄

Windows Autostart & Tray Mode

Runs automatically in the background when your PC boots. Closing the window minimizes it to the Windows System Tray, keeping the server alive 24x7.

⚙️

Full Print Controls

Customize copies, color vs monochrome, paper size (A4, 4x6 Photo, Legal), orientation, duplex (double-sided), and fit-to-page scaling right from your phone.

📲

Direct Android Share Target

Integrates seamlessly into the Android system share sheet. Send photos directly from Google Photos, Gallery, WhatsApp, or File Manager in 1 tap.

Simple Setup

Get Started in Under 2 Minutes

No complicated network setup or technical skills required.

1

Install PC Server

Download and run Print_Ofo_Setup.exe on your Windows 10/11 laptop or desktop connected to your USB printer. It starts instantly.

2

Install Android App

Download Print Ofo Mobile onto your phone. Connect phone and PC to the same Wi-Fi (or mobile hotspot), and scan the QR code once.

3

Print Any Document

Open any photo or PDF on your phone, tap Share > Print Ofo, choose your printer settings, and hit Print Now!

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about setting up and using Print Ofo.

Q: Does Print Ofo need an active internet connection?
No. Print Ofo operates 100% locally on your Wi-Fi router or laptop mobile hotspot. No internet bandwidth is consumed and your documents never leave your local network.
Q: Do I need to pair my phone with a QR code every time?
No. You only scan the QR code once during the initial pairing. After that, both your PC and Android phone remember each other and auto-reconnect dynamically whenever connected to the same Wi-Fi.
Q: What happens when I close the Windows app window?
Clicking the close button ('X') minimizes the application to the Windows System Tray (notification area near your taskbar clock). The print server continues running 24/7 so your mobile phone can print at any time.
Q: Does the PC server start automatically when my computer boots?
Yes! Version 1.1.0 includes automatic Windows startup registration. Whenever you turn on or restart your PC, the Print Ofo server starts silently in the background.
Q: Is my print data stored or tracked anywhere?
Never. Print Ofo has zero cloud analytics, zero user telemetry, and zero tracking servers. Documents stream directly into the local Windows Spooler and are cleared upon printing.
