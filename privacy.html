Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

At Print Ofo, user privacy is our foundational principle. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when using the Print Ofo Windows Server and the Print Ofo Android Application.

1. 100% Local Network Operation (Zero Cloud Servers)

Print Ofo is engineered to work exclusively within your local Wi-Fi or LAN network. We do not operate external cloud servers, document relays, or telemetry databases.

  • No File Uploads to the Internet: Photos, documents, and print data sent from your phone stream directly to your Windows PC over local network sockets (HTTP/HTTPS/UDP).
  • No Accounts or Sign-ups: You are never required to provide your name, email, phone number, or credentials to use Print Ofo.

2. Data Collected & Stored Locally

The only data stored is strictly local to your devices:

  • Pairing Token: A cryptographically generated token stored locally on your PC and Android device to authorize print requests.
  • Last Known IP Address: Saved in your phone's local storage (`SharedPreferences`) to allow dynamic auto-reconnecting on your Wi-Fi network.
  • Spooler Temporary Buffer: When a document is received by your PC, it is written to a temporary local spool file and passed to the native Windows Print Spooler. Once the job completes, temporary buffers are removed.

3. Permissions Used by Android Application

  • Wi-Fi / Network State: Needed to detect if the phone is connected to the same local Wi-Fi as your PC.
  • Camera Permission (Optional): Only used to scan the pairing QR code displayed on your PC screen. No photos or video frames are saved or transmitted.
  • Storage / Media Access: Needed when you choose to select a photo or PDF file from your phone storage to print.

4. Third-Party Services & Tracking

Print Ofo contains no third-party analytics SDKs, no advertisement trackers, and no telemetry services. Your usage data is never sold or shared with any third party.
